# Precedent — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

Precedent is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian").

**What it stores.** For each installation, Precedent stores in Forge storage: decisions captured from Confluence pages, Jira comments or Rovo conversations (statement, title, rationale, owner name or Atlassian account id, dates, source links, tags, guardrail terms, exceptions and history), and the latest conflict-check result for Jira work items (key, summary, project, status, assignee display name, evidence sentence). It also writes a `precedent` issue property on checked work items so they can be found with JQL.

**What it does not do.** Precedent does not send any data outside Atlassian's infrastructure, does not use third-party services or analytics, does not call external AI models, and does not sell or share data.

**Who can see it.** Data is visible to users of the site through the app's Jira and Confluence surfaces and through Rovo / MCP tools, subject to those products' permissions.

**Retention and deletion.** Data is kept until it is deleted in the app or the app is uninstalled; uninstalling removes the app's Forge storage according to Atlassian's Forge data retention policy.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/qybaihe/precedent/issues.
